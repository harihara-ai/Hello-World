## Root causes behind the new findings

- RC1: the gate trusts local state the agent controls and diff_scope cannot see (CLI `--base`, HEAD fallback, `.git/config`, `.git/info/attributes`, `__pycache__`). R2-2-01, 04, 05.
- RC2: the human floor is checked against the worktree, not the base commit. R2-2-02.
- RC3: plan and evidence are re-read at done from the editable, diff_scope-exempt change folder (Type, impact scan, coverage report, baseline). R2-2-03, 06, 08.
- RC4: two hand lists of "files that steer the agent or the gates" (kitconst.PROTECTED, tangle HIGH_RISK) that drift. R2-2-07.
- RC5: forge --check compares decoded text, not bytes. R2-2-09, 10 (parser state, minor).
